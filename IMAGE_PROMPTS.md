# Rebelle campaign image direction

Original AI-generated assets live in `public/images/`. The prompts below are reproducible direction for a future photo or image-production pass. Keep all imagery unbranded except for an original angular R patch. No copied energy-drink iconography.

## Hero portrait

Wide horizontal luxury streetwear campaign photograph. A dark-skinned model centered to the right in an oversized ice-silver technical puffer with a sculptural protective hood and mirrored ski visor. Slate-blue studio atmosphere, fine cold mist, a long cool rim light, subtle streaks suggesting speed behind the figure. Hyper-real fabric sheen and seam detail. Strong negative space on the left for editorial typography. Fashion campaign art direction, restrained and premium. No text, no existing logos, no beverage branding.

## Studio product sequence

Four separate full-front product photographs on a consistent muted blue-grey seamless background, balanced softbox light, true-to-life nylon texture, clean shadows, and accurate construction details: (1) metallic silver high-volume puffer with protective hood; (2) midnight navy insulated puffer with angular quilting and reflective piping; (3) frost-white long shell with translucent ice-blue panels; (4) steel-grey wide cargo trousers with technical pockets and reflective contour seams. Tiny original angular R patch where relevant. No models, text, or borrowed logos.

## Alpine campaign

Wide cinematic alpine landscape in deep steel-blue twilight. Jagged snow-covered mountain ridge, wind-blown snow, atmospheric depth, cold moving light across the sky. Quiet but powerful. Leave the left portion dark enough for large white type. No people, text, logos, or recognizable commercial marks.

## Expanded capsule

Five additional original images extend the same cold studio language: (1) a cropped silver insulated vest photographed from the front; (2) an oversized frost-marl thermal knit with visible ribbing; (3) a modular slate-blue crossbody sling with technical straps; (4) sculptural silver winter gloves with articulated panels; and (5) a cinematic night-city campaign portrait of a model wearing the capsule. Use cool blue-grey light, soft but precise highlights, honest fabric detail, and clean silhouettes. Keep the garment images on a consistent seamless backdrop, with no text or recognizable commercial logos.

## Production notes

Export WebP for the site; preserve full-resolution originals off-site for future artwork. Check seams, zippers, hands, and logotypes closely before print or commercial use. The images are concept visuals, not photographs of physical inventory.
