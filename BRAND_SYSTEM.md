# Rebelle — brand system

## Positioning

**Rebelle** is a cold-weather streetwear concept about freedom of movement. It takes the speed and after-dark attitude of the original Rebelle seed into a colder, more technical world. The first capsule, **Shift / 01**, joins oversized protection with precise lines and a glint of silver. This is an independent Kout Project portfolio brand, not a collaboration or licensed product.

**Promise:** Move beyond the weather.

**Audience:** Style-led people who move between city nights, travel, and outdoor spaces and want technical clothing with a strong silhouette.

**Voice:** Short, active, direct. Talk about what a garment lets someone do. Avoid invented performance claims and empty luxury language.

## Identity

The wordmark is a heavy, condensed uppercase `REBELLE`. The **R/slash** mark is drawn from an original block R with a diagonal interruption: a visual cue for changing direction. It may appear small on a garment, in navigation, or as a campaign signature. Do not use wings, bulls, or marks from existing energy brands. The studio image may contain an AI-rendered approximate R patch; the authoritative symbol is the SVG in the site component.

The earlier scarlet racing accent is deliberately retired for this cold capsule. Motion remains in the diagonal slash, sharp crop, and silver reflective language.

**Type:** Condensed industrial display (`Impact` with `Arial Narrow` fallback) for campaign statements and wordmark; plain Arial/Helvetica for details and controls. Functional labels are uppercase and tightly spaced; paragraph copy remains sentence case.

**Composition:** One cinematic close crop, a uniform four-column capsule grid, a city-night campaign image, and a wide alpine statement. The site alternates intimate apparel texture with scale and distance. Use generous dark space so the silver pieces carry the visual weight.

## Interface rules

- Keep utility information legible against imagery; do not place long copy over detailed garment areas.
- Use cropped campaign photography for emotion and clean studio photography for product clarity.
- Interaction is user-triggered: product reveal on hover, category filters, sort, size selection, bag feedback, and checkout steps. Do not animate every section on scroll.
- A product's dedicated page carries fit, construction, and an editorial pairing. The landing and collection pages use equal product tiles in the original four-column rhythm, becoming two columns on small screens.
- The Contact page gives an inquiry form preview and an actual link to the creator's GitHub profile. Do not imply the form sends a message.
- Keep reduced-motion support, visible focus, and mobile navigation.
- Do not reproduce the reference artwork, logo, copy, or exact layout. Its cold palette, close-cropped outerwear, compact utility text, and alpine scale are translated into an original composition.

## Extensions

Future campaigns can move from blue twilight into other temperature worlds while retaining the R/slash and motion-led voice. A real shop would need validated product specifications, stock, fulfillment, policies, a contact endpoint, and a payment provider.
